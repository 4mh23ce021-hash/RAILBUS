# RAILBUS — Project Overview & Mobility Blueprint

## Tagline
> **"Your Train Arrives. Your City Connects."**

## Product Identity
**RAILBUS — INTELLIGENT MOBILITY NETWORK**

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Every day, thousands of passengers arrive at **Mysuru Junction Railway Station (MYS)** on express and superfast trains like the Shatabdi Express, Vande Bharat Express, and Chamundi Express. 

Upon deboarding, passengers face severe first-and-last-mile connectivity issues:
1. Long queues for auto-rickshaws and surge-priced taxis.
2. Uncoordinated public city buses departing empty or arriving after passengers have already dispersed.
3. Lack of direct, guaranteed transit to major hubs like Kuvempunagar, Hebbal Industrial IT Area, Manasagangothri, Vijayanagar, and the Suburban Bus Stand.

---

## 2. The RAILBUS Solution
RAILBUS connects arriving rail passengers with existing KSRTC city bus services through dynamic passenger pooling and real-time train telemetry synchronization:

1. **Pre-Arrival Booking**: Passengers select their inbound train and city destination before arriving in Mysuru.
2. **Dynamic Pooling Engine**: Compatible passengers traveling on the same train to the same corridor are grouped into dedicated pools (minimum 20, maximum 56 capacity).
3. **Admin Operations Gateway**: The Admin Command Center reviews demand density, assigns available KSRTC fleet vehicles, and schedules direct departures from Station Portico Bays 3 & 4.
4. **Digital Boarding & Ticketing**: Passengers receive digital tickets with high-density QR codes and 6-digit confirmation numbers. Conductor validation occurs in seconds at the platform portico.
